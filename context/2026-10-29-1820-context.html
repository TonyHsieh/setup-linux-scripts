📚 Kavita Storage & Volume Mount Architecture

Clarified persistent storage mechanics in docker-compose.yml:

  • Persistent Mount: Kavita maps ${BOOKS_DIR:-$HOME/Books}:/books:ro. Drives do not need to be re-mounted each time; mounts persist permanently across restarts.
  • Single / Custom Drive: Users can override BOOKS_DIR=/path/to/drive in local-services/.env without touching Compose code.
  • Multiple Disparate Drives: Additional storage volumes can be added directly under kavita.volumes (e.g., /mnt/manga:/manga:ro, /mnt/nas:/nas:ro) and browseable inside Kavita's library picker.
